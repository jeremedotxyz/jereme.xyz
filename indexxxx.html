Operating note

Not a holding company. More like a shared nervous system.

The same taste, technical judgment, and refusal to fake certainty moves across every page: from LiDAR corridor safety and evaluation harnesses to chairs chosen on purpose, material stories, and slower physical spaces. The medium changes. The standard doesn’t.

This is less about scale for its own sake and more about building a body of work that can be scrutinized closely and still feel deliberate. Useful and unhurried. Clear enough to ship. Quiet enough to last.
