Industrial Design / Concept
Monoblock Aluminum Toaster
fw/r × nrthrn · Scale 1:3 · Rev A · 2026

A toaster machined from a single billet of 6061-T6 aluminum. No seams, no panels — just a continuous radiused volume with a brushed bead-blast finish and clear hard anodize. All electrical and thermal components live inside a certified replaceable ceramic cartridge so the exterior never has to be touched again. One tactile control. One hidden crumb pull. Service from the bottom plate only.

Body
Single 6061-T6 aluminum billet. Brushed bead-blast finish, clear hard anodize. No seams, no panel joins.
Thermal Isolation
Ceramic-lined replaceable cartridge with air gaps. The outer shell is never a structural heating element.
Controls
One flush rotary push on the right side. Browning status via a recessed light line on the front.
Service
Bottom plate only. Exposes cartridge fasteners, crumb tray, and strain relief. Exterior silhouette unchanged.
Status
Concept schematic — Rev A · 2026. Not a manufacturing specification.
