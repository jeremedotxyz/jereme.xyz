LiDAR.

The light that runs out ahead and tells you what's there. Sensing work from fw/r — point clouds, terrain, reliability over approximation.

LiDAR is the discipline of asking a beam of light to come back honest. We work on the parts of that question that don't make the press release — sensor selection, calibration, registration, fusion, the geometry of being sure.

Deployments span infrastructure, terrain capture, robotics perception, and field environments where GPS is unreliable and the answer still has to hold. Boutique by design. Called in when the obvious has been tried.
