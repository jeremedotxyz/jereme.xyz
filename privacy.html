Privacy Policy

effective: whenever you landed here · last updated: probably not recently enough

01 / The Short Version

This is a personal website. Not a SaaS. Not a startup. Not an app collecting your data to sell you sneakers. It's just a site about me, what I'm building, and what I'm thinking about. Cool? Cool.

I don't have a data team. I don't have a privacy officer. I have a laptop and too many tabs open. Act accordingly.

02 / What I Collect

Basically nothing intentionally. If you fill out the contact form (the "signal" section), I receive your name, email, and whatever you typed. That goes to my inbox. I might reply. I will definitely read it.

If you're behind any analytics that auto-fire (looking at you, referral headers), I might see aggregate traffic data — no names, no faces, just numbers that tell me whether anyone besides my mom visits this site. Spoiler: sometimes yes.

03 / What I Don't Collect

Passwords (there's no login). Payment info (there's nothing to buy). Your location beyond what a server log naturally knows. Your browsing history. Your secrets. Your startup idea — keep that one to yourself.

04 / Third-Party Stuff

This site links out to social profiles, projects, and ventures I'm involved in. Once you leave jereme.xyz, you're on their turf with their rules. I have no control over what they collect and I'm not responsible for it. Read their privacy policies. Or don't. I'm not your dad.

If I'm using embedded fonts, analytics, or a CDN, those providers have their own data policies. Again, not a data broker operation here — just trying to keep the site from looking like it was built in 2003.

05 / Cookies

Probably minimal to none. I'm not running e-commerce or a membership system. If a cookie sneaks in from a third-party embed, that's on them and you can block it at your browser level without losing a single thing this site offers.

06 / Your Data, Your Rights

Want me to delete the email you sent? Ask. Want to know what I have on you? Also ask — fair warning, the answer is probably "your email address and a nice message you sent once." GDPR folks: I see you. CCPA folks: likewise. Email me and we'll sort it out like adults.

07 / Updates to This Policy

If something material changes, I'll update this page. I won't send you an email about it because I probably don't have your email. The "last updated" date at the top will change. That's the extent of the notification system here.
