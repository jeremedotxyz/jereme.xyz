§ 1

Definitions and Interpretive Framework

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them herein, unless context otherwise dictates, common sense prevails, or the reader is a lawyer in which case: hello, there is nothing here for you.

The Site refers to jereme.xyz, a personal website. Not a platform. Not a marketplace. Not a SaaS product with a free tier and an aggressive upgrade prompt. A website. About a person.

§ 1.2 — "The Operator"

The Operator refers to Jereme Abernathy, a human being located somewhere in the Pacific Northwest, operating this site on a single domain, with no venture funding, no data team, and no privacy officer on retainer.

§ 1.3 — "You" or "The User"

You refers to any person, entity, automated system, or aggressively curious bot that has navigated to this URL. Welcome. Please behave.

§ 1.4 — "Personal Data"

Personal Data means any information that could identify you as a specific individual. On this site, that is largely limited to whatever you voluntarily type into the contact form, because there is nothing else that would generate it.

§ 1.5 — "The Ventures"

The Ventures refers collectively to fw/r, pine, nrthrn, and drift — independent commercial and creative entities linked from The Site but operating under entirely separate governance, infrastructure, and legal standing. See § 8 for the full scope limitation on this, which is important and not just filler.

§ 2

Nature and Scope of This Document

§ 2.1 — What This Is

This is a Privacy Policy for a personal website. It exists because the internet decided that every website, regardless of whether it collects a single byte of personal data, must have one of these. The Operator respects that convention while also noting, for the record, that it is a lot of words for "I don't really collect your data."

§ 2.2 — What This Is Not

This document is not a data processing agreement. It is not a GDPR Article 30 record of processing activities. It is not a BAA. It is not applicable to any commercial transaction, because there are no commercial transactions available on this site. If you are looking for a privacy policy for one of The Ventures, you are on the wrong page. Those entities maintain their own documentation. See § 8.

In summary: this is a personal website. The Operator is not Meta. The data infrastructure is not Meta's. The budget is also not Meta's. Manage expectations accordingly.

§ 3

Information Collected

The following categories of information may be collected through use of The Site, enumerated here with full transparency and a certain amount of resignation that this requires enumeration at all:

§ 3.1 — Contact Form Submissions

If You complete and submit the contact form (referred to on the site as the "signal" section), The Operator receives: your name, your email address, and the content of your message. This information is transmitted to The Operator's inbox via Resend, a transactional email service, and to a designated Slack channel for response tracking.

This data is used exclusively for the purpose of responding to you. It is not sold. It is not passed to advertisers. It is not used to build a profile. It is used to write you back, possibly within the same business day, probably with fewer words than you might expect.

§ 3.2 — Analytics and Traffic Data

The Site uses Google Analytics (GA4) to collect aggregate, anonymized traffic data including page views, session durations, geographic regions at the country or city level, device types, and referral sources. This data does not include your name, your email, or any individually identifying information. It tells The Operator things like "someone from Vancouver, WA spent four minutes on the site," not "that was Derek and here is his home address."

§ 3.3 — Server and Edge Logs

The Site is served through Cloudflare's infrastructure. Cloudflare may log standard server request data including IP addresses, request timestamps, and browser user agents as part of its normal operation. This is governed by Cloudflare's own Privacy Policy, not this one. The Operator has access to aggregate Cloudflare analytics but does not actively monitor individual request logs.

§ 3.4 — User Preference Data

The Site stores your light/dark mode preference in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted anywhere. It exists solely so the site does not forget you prefer dark mode every time you close a tab, which would be annoying for everyone.

§ 4

Information Not Collected

For the avoidance of doubt, and because this seems to comfort people, the following categories of information are explicitly not collected by The Site:

  • Passwords — there is no login system. There is nothing to log into. This is a website, not an app.
  • Payment information — there is nothing to purchase. Please do not attempt to give this site money.
  • Biometric data — there are no cameras embedded in the website. This is not a Marvel villain origin story.
  • Health or medical information — not applicable. The Operator is not a healthcare provider.
  • Social Security numbers, government identification, or financial account details — what are you doing here.
  • Your precise geolocation — the site knows what country you're probably in. That is the end of its geographic ambition.
  • Your browsing history outside of this domain — The Operator is not interested. Please stop visiting weird sites.
  • Your contacts, photos, microphone input, or camera feed — see note above re: Marvel villain.
  • Your startup idea — although honestly you should be more protective of it elsewhere on the internet.

§ 6

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

§ 6.1 — First-Party Storage

The Site uses browser local storage (not cookies, technically, but worth mentioning) to retain your theme preference (light or dark mode) between sessions. This is described in § 3.4. This data does not leave your device and The Operator cannot access it.

§ 6.2 — Analytics Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies to measure aggregate site usage as described in § 3.2. These are measurement cookies, not advertising cookies. They are used to count visits, not to follow You around the internet showing You ads for things you looked at one time six months ago.

§ 6.3 — Cookie Blocking

You may block, delete, or otherwise interfere with cookies at the browser level using any number of freely available tools and browser settings. Doing so will not break this site. The site will continue to function normally. The only consequence is that The Operator will have slightly less aggregate traffic data, which is fine.

§ 7

Data Subject Rights

If You are covered by the GDPR, CCPA, or any other data protection regulation that grants you rights with respect to your personal data, those rights are acknowledged here. Specifically:

§ 7.1 — Right of Access

You may request to know what personal data The Operator holds about you. The answer is likely: your email address and a message you sent at some point. The Operator will confirm this within a reasonable timeframe, defined here as "before the heat death of the universe, probably much sooner."

§ 7.2 — Right to Erasure

You may request deletion of any personal data The Operator holds. This request will be honored. Email and request it. The Operator will delete it. There will be no argument. There is no retention policy to cite as justification for keeping it.

§ 7.3 — Right to Object

You may object to any processing of your data. Given the minimal processing described in § 3, this right will be very easy to exercise and very easy to honor.

§ 7.4 — How to Exercise These Rights

Email The Operator at the address found in the contact section of The Site. State what you want. The Operator will respond. This is the entire process. There is no ticket system. There is no data protection officer portal. There is email.
