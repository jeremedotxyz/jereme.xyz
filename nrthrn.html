nrthrn

(est. 2025)

designed objects and material ideas with a cleaner, more restrained physical language.

nrthrn is mocked as an object-led case study: less lifestyle language, more restraint, more surface area for form, finish, texture, and photographed detail. The structure borrows directly from the Foundation Labs project pages.

This format works well once there are real product shots, packaging, prototypes, or installation images. The layout already has enough hierarchy to feel intentional even before the final work exists.
