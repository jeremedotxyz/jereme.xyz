02 / Practice

One operating instinct. Four very different surfaces.

Each venture stands on its own. The common thread is physical consequence: the system has to work, the object has to earn its place, and the room has to feel considered.

Robotics / LiDAR / applied AI

Find the wires. Build the rig. Make it work.

fw/r gets called when somebody tried the obvious and the obvious did not hold. Boutique by design, close to the field, allergic to innovation theater.

Hospitality / pace / return

A room where someone chose the chairs on purpose.

pine is built as a room first: useful in the morning, generous in the middle of the day, and calm enough that people remember how it felt.

Product / material / use

Every millimeter gets a reason.

nrthrn develops consumer and industrial goods with real materials, real weight, and an insistence that objects earn their place on a counter or shelf.

Coast / patience / place

Smaller, slower, more salt in the air.

drift is pine's little sister: a quieter coastal room still choosing its block, moving at the speed the place asks of it.

03 / Operating method

Start with what is actually there. Then make the strange thing legible.

01

Name the real problem.

Good work usually begins when someone says "I don't know" out loud and means it. Strip away the performance of certainty and find the constraint that is actually shaping the outcome.

02

Pressure-test the direction.

Put the idea against sensors, signals, agents, hardware, public infrastructure, messy environments, real materials, service flow, and people with lives to get back to.

03

Leave behind something durable.

A system that behaves in the field. An object that makes sense in the hand. A room that resets itself. Language clear enough to survive after the meeting ends.

05 / Point of view

I'm not chasing scale. I'm chasing a body of work.

What I make tends to come out useful and unhurried: the speed of a beam of light returning with an exact measurement, a piece of hardware that earns its place, a quiet thing in a noisy room.

I am interested in being good in the spots where I actually show up. Some of this work is mine. Most of it will not be. That is the job.
