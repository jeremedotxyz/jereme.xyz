is pine open?

not yet. it's got a very long runway and i have a lot of decisions to make about fixtures. i'd rather get the room right than pretend the opening date is a personality.

why all the different things?

different materials, same attention. a sensor, a room, and an object all have to make sense to the person living with them.

can i send you something half-formed?

absolutely not. just kidding. half-formed is fine. tell me what you’re thinking, where you’re stuck, and what you’ve tried. we can start there.
