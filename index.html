Method / Not a framework, relax

Turn the fog into a buildable map.

The job is to bring enough clarity, taste, technical judgment, and operator pressure to make the next move obvious.

  • 01
    Find the real problem.

    Separate the actual system from the deck, the meeting, the vibe, and the wishful thinking.

  • 02
    Make behavior measurable.

    Define what should happen, what can break, what needs evidence, and how the work earns trust.

  • 03
    Shape the thing.

    Turn strategy into prototypes, operating logic, product direction, technical narrative, or the first version worth testing.

  • 04
    Keep the room honest.

    Clear language. Good taste. No fake certainty. No decorative complexity. No pretending the emperor is dressed.
