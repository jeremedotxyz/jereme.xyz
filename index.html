05 / point of view

The work is quieter than the discourse. On purpose.

There is a lot of aesthetic theater in the world right now: product language pretending to be strategy, metrics pretending to be taste, and AI discourse pretending to be understanding. The portfolio is built in reaction to that.

Good work does not need to shout to prove it has a point of view. It needs to be specific, legible, durable, and strange in the right places.

This is not about being everywhere. It is about being good in the spots that actually matter.

Bring the fuzzy thing.

Technical, physical, future-facing, hard to explain. The useful conversation usually begins right there.
