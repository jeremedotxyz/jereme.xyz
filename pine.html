pine

(est. 2022*)

a slower local-world concept shaped around coffee, atmosphere, and neighborhood ritual.

pine is framed here like a Foundation Labs app page, but with a warmer material language. The goal is not a cafe website; it is a world-building case study for the visual, spatial, and ritual system around the project.

The mock page uses fewer words, larger surfaces, and stronger shapes so future photography, menus, signage, packaging, or interiors can slide in without changing the structure.
