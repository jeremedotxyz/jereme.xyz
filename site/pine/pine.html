Subject: Operating Brief Ref: FOLKLORE_&_CHILL

At pine, we had a radical thought: what if we didn’t passive-aggressively tap our watches while you dared to enjoy a second hour of productivity? Wild, we know.

So we cherry-picked chairs comfortable enough to make you question whether you accidentally moved in. Our Wi-Fi is fast enough to make buffering feel like a rumor from the old country. And when that first cup is empty? Surprise — round two’s on us.

Not because we’re saints, but because your extended stay means we’re doing the coffee shop thing right.

pine is built for lingering. For laptop days. For accidental three-hour conversations. For reading one chapter and somehow staying until the light changes. We’ve got hot coffee, local snacks, enough sarcasm to keep the room honest, and a vibe that says, yes, you can absolutely be the main character here.

And sure, pine has a way of attracting the in-crowd. The interesting ones. The quietly cool ones. The people who somehow make sitting in a chair look intentional.

We’re not saying your ex will hear you’re thriving here. But we’re also not in charge of folklore. Come for the coffee. Stay for the snacks. Camp out in the chair. Finish the thing. Start the thing. Pretend to work while actually staring dramatically out the window.

In short: we’re pine, and we fully support your plot twist.
