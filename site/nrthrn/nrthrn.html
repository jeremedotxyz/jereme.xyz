Design practice

Less styling. More consequence.

A product is not just a shape. It is a chain of decisions: material, assembly, use, repair, price, story, shelf, hand, and what the thing quietly teaches the person using it.

  • 01
    Clarify the role.

    Name what the object does, what it refuses to do, where it lives, and why someone would keep it.

  • 02
    Prototype the behavior.

    Explore form, interface, tactility, materials, and repeat use before mistaking a pretty render for a product.

  • 03
    Build the product language.

    Shape the visual system, naming, proportions, components, and story so the object feels like it belongs to a world.

  • 04
    Respect the constraints.

    Manufacturing, sourcing, packaging, maintenance, and cost are not boring details. They are where the design becomes real.
