narwhal

Narwhal is fw/r's below surface LiDAR system, built to make submerged environments visible, measurable, and inspectable where cameras and surface assumptions run out. Perception, taken below sea level.

Perception for the corridor. Language systems for the decisions. Build rigs for everything between.

fw/r works where software has to negotiate with hardware, public infrastructure, messy environments, and the delightful horror of reality refusing to behave like a clean demo.
