fw/r

(est. 2019)

intelligent systems, robotics experiments, and buildable technical infrastructure for strange physical futures.

fw/r is the systems-facing wing of the portfolio: robotics, applied AI, sensing, prototyping, and the machinery required to turn abstract technical direction into something that can actually ship. Less innovation theater, more finding the wires, naming the ghost, building the rig, and making the weird thing work.

It is where vague future-facing ideas get pressure-tested against actual constraints: sensors, signals, agents, hardware, public infrastructure, messy environments, and the delightful horror of reality refusing to behave like a clean demo.

Explore more on the official site at fw/r.
