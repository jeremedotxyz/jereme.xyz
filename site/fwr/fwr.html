Questions

What fails in the field?

Transit, public infrastructure, hardware, and messy environments are the places where technical narratives either earn their keep or fall apart.

Method

Instrument, test, iterate, ship.

The work gets specific through prototypes, evaluation loops, scenario suites, and surfaces that expose what the system is actually doing.

Tone

Clear enough to survive contact with reality.

Quiet by design, but not vague. Taste is not there to soften the work. It is there to make the work legible and durable.

Bring the technical thing that still needs a room to think in.

If the problem is stuck between systems strategy and actual implementation, that is exactly where fw/r starts to get useful.
